OUR STORY
LaBrie

LaBrie was born from a simple idea — that fragrance can be more than a scent. It can be a memory, a feeling, a part of your identity. The brand was founded in Paris by a group of creatives and perfumers who shared a vision: to create unique, emotional and timeless fragrances

Today, La Brie is a global perfume house, combining traditional craftsmanship with a modern vision. Our perfumes are inspired by the beauty of the world — its cities, people, nature and moments that stay with us forever.
